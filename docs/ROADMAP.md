# Roadmap

Planned work after 1.2. Patch versions (1.3.x) carry fixes and anything that slips from the minor release.

## 1.3

* **Command palette (Ctrl+K)**: type an account, a game, a page or an action and press Enter.
* **Scheduled launch**: launch a game on a given account at a set time, online or in ghost mode.
* **Discord Rich Presence**: game and champion or agent icon, match timer, rank, Riot ID (optional), live score (team kills on League of Legends, rounds on VALORANT), queue type. Turned off automatically in ghost mode.
* **League of Legends Mythic Shop**: current rotation with its countdown.
* **Patch recap**: buffs, nerfs and adjustments for League of Legends, TFT and VALORANT, inside the app.
* **Usage statistics**: anonymous and opt-in, sent to Aptabase (EU).
* **Config transfer**: copy keybinds and settings from one account to another, export and import by file, short code or QR code.
* **Session reuse**: reuse an account's "Stay signed in" Riot session to switch without a captcha when Riot allows it.

## 1.4

* **macOS** (League of Legends and TFT).
* **Session recap** when the game closes: time played, matches, LP or RR change.
* **Riot server status** per region, shown before launching.
* **Account statistics** per game and per week (time in game and in the launcher).
* **Decay alert**: banner on the accounts page when an account gets close to losing LP.
* **Automatic encrypted backup** of the vault to a folder of your choice.
* **Shards rework**: more things to unlock with the shards earned in the app.

## Later

* VALORANT store and Night Market (opt-in, with a risk warning).
* Esport hub: matches of the day and results.
* More languages: Portuguese (Brazil), Polish, Turkish, Russian, Korean, Japanese, Traditional Chinese, Vietnamese, Arabic (right to left).
* Multi-account friends view.
* Reward expiry reminders, "new season" profile, coach and LAN mode.
* External review of the vault.
